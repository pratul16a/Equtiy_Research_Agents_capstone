# Indian Stock Market Guide

## Market Structure

### NSE (National Stock Exchange)
- India's largest stock exchange by volume
- Benchmark index: Nifty 50 (top 50 companies by market cap)
- Other indices: Nifty Bank, Nifty IT, Nifty Midcap 100, Nifty Smallcap 250
- yfinance suffix: .NS

### BSE (Bombay Stock Exchange)
- Asia's oldest stock exchange (established 1875)
- Benchmark index: Sensex (top 30 companies)
- yfinance suffix: .BO
- Generally lower volume than NSE for most stocks

### Trading Hours
- Pre-open session: 9:00 AM - 9:15 AM IST
- Regular trading: 9:15 AM - 3:30 PM IST
- Post-close session: 3:30 PM - 4:00 PM IST

## Settlement Cycle
- T+1 settlement (effective from January 2023)
- Previously T+2; India was one of the first major markets to adopt T+1

## Circuit Limits
- Individual stocks: 5%, 10%, or 20% based on exchange criteria
- Index circuit breakers: 10%, 15%, 20% decline from previous close
- Upper circuit = no sellers (bullish), Lower circuit = no buyers (bearish)

## Indian Financial Year
- April 1 to March 31
- FY25 = April 1, 2024 to March 31, 2025
- Quarterly results: Q1 (Apr-Jun), Q2 (Jul-Sep), Q3 (Oct-Dec), Q4 (Jan-Mar)
- Annual results announced: May-June (for previous FY)

## Key Market Participants

### Foreign Institutional Investors (FII/FPI)
- Largest non-promoter holders in most large-cap stocks
- Net FII flows are the single biggest driver of short-term market direction
- Track daily FII/DII data from NSE website
- FII selling often coincides with rupee depreciation

### Domestic Institutional Investors (DII)
- Includes mutual funds, insurance companies (LIC), pension funds
- SIP (Systematic Investment Plan) flows have become a stabilizing force
- Monthly SIP flows: ~Rs 20,000+ Crores (growing consistently)
- DIIs often buy when FIIs sell, providing a floor

### Retail Investors
- India has ~13+ crore demat accounts (growing rapidly post-COVID)
- Retail activity is concentrated in mid/small caps
- High retail holding can increase volatility

## Tax Considerations
- **LTCG (Long Term Capital Gains)**: 12.5% on gains above Rs 1.25 lakh (holding > 1 year)
- **STCG (Short Term Capital Gains)**: 20% (holding < 1 year)
- **Dividend Tax**: Taxable in hands of investor at slab rate
- **STT (Securities Transaction Tax)**: 0.1% on buy and sell of equity delivery

## Key Economic Indicators for Indian Markets
1. **RBI Repo Rate**: Currently ~6.5% (monetary policy driver)
2. **CPI Inflation**: RBI targets 4% (+/- 2%)
3. **GDP Growth**: India growing at ~6-7% (fastest major economy)
4. **Rupee/USD Exchange Rate**: Impacts IT companies, importers/exporters
5. **Crude Oil Price**: India imports ~80% of crude, higher prices are negative
6. **Monsoon**: Critical for rural economy, affects FMCG, auto demand
7. **GST Collections**: Proxy for economic activity
