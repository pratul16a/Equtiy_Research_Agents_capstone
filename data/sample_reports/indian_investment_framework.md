# Indian Equity Investment Framework

## Valuation Benchmarks for Indian Markets

### Nifty 50 Historical P/E Bands
- **Deeply Undervalued**: P/E < 15x (rare, last seen during March 2020 crash)
- **Undervalued**: P/E 15-18x (strong buying opportunity)
- **Fair Value**: P/E 18-22x (reasonable for long-term investors)
- **Slightly Overvalued**: P/E 22-25x (proceed with caution)
- **Overvalued**: P/E > 25x (elevated risk, selective approach needed)
- Historical mean Nifty P/E: ~20x over the last 20 years

### Sector-Wise P/E Norms (Indian Market)
- **IT Services**: 25-35x (high quality, predictable cash flows)
- **Private Banks**: 18-30x (depends on asset quality and growth)
- **PSU Banks**: 8-15x (lower valuations due to governance concerns)
- **FMCG**: 40-65x (premium for brand moats and consistency)
- **Pharma**: 20-35x (depends on US/India revenue mix)
- **Auto**: 15-30x (cyclical, depends on cycle position)
- **Metals**: 8-15x (deeply cyclical, use EV/EBITDA)
- **Real Estate**: 15-25x (use NAV-based valuation)

## ROCE Benchmarks by Indian Sector
Return on Capital Employed is the single most important metric for Indian companies.

- **Excellent (>25%)**: IT services (TCS, Infosys), FMCG (HUL, Nestle), Consumer brands
- **Good (15-25%)**: Private banks, specialty chemicals, auto components
- **Average (10-15%)**: Capital goods, pharma, diversified conglomerates
- **Below Average (<10%)**: Metals, real estate, PSU companies, capital-intensive industries

## Promoter Holding Guidelines
Promoter holding is unique to Indian markets and a key governance indicator.

### Healthy Promoter Holding
- **>60%**: Strong promoter commitment, typical of family-run businesses
- **40-60%**: Balanced — good governance with adequate public float
- **30-40%**: Acceptable but monitor for any reduction
- **<30%**: Concern — could signal lack of promoter confidence or upcoming stake sale

### Promoter Pledge Red Flags
- **<5% pledged**: Healthy, no concern
- **5-15% pledged**: Monitor — promoter may need financing
- **15-30% pledged**: Warning — significant financial stress possible
- **>30% pledged**: High risk — potential forced selling, avoid or underweight

## FII/DII Flow Interpretation
- **FII buying + DII buying**: Strongly bullish, broad-based conviction
- **FII buying + DII selling**: Bullish with rotation, FII-driven rally
- **FII selling + DII buying**: Neutral to bullish, domestic conviction holding up
- **FII selling + DII selling**: Bearish, broad risk-off sentiment

## Indian Market Seasonality
- **Budget Rally** (Jan-Feb): Markets often rally ahead of Union Budget
- **Earnings Season**: Q1 (Jul-Aug), Q2 (Oct-Nov), Q3 (Jan-Feb), Q4 (Apr-May)
- **Diwali/Muhurat Trading** (Oct-Nov): Traditionally bullish
- **Tax Loss Harvesting** (Mar): Selling pressure in March for tax optimization
- **Monsoon Impact**: Critical for rural demand — affects FMCG, auto, agri stocks

## Key Regulatory Bodies
- **SEBI**: Securities and Exchange Board of India (market regulator)
- **RBI**: Reserve Bank of India (monetary policy, banking regulator)
- **MCA**: Ministry of Corporate Affairs (company law compliance)
- **NSE/BSE**: Primary stock exchanges
